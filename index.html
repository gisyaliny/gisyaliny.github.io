About me

Yalin Yang, Ph.D.

杨亚霖

I’m a research scientist and GIS programmer at the West Virginia GIS Technical Center at West Virginia University.

I study how the built environment shapes social events and human networks. My work brings spatial analysis, machine learning and cloud computing to problems ranging from urban image segmentation to community flood risk.
