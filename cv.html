Selected GIS & GeoAI projects

West Virginia Flood Resiliency Framework
I built the entire website, front end and back end, to turn flood-risk data from our data scientists into maps and reports that communities can use.

Prithvi burn-scar mapping
I deployed Prithvi-100M on I-GUIDE and ran the fine-tuning pipeline that turns six-band satellite imagery into burn-scar masks.

River valley profile add-in
I extended a C# ArcMap add-in so researchers can trace valley floors and extract cross-sections from LiDAR elevation grids.

Footprints GPS collection
I built the Android recorder and AWS backend that let study participants share GPS updates and pause recording whenever they choose.
